THE HISTORY OF APEX RIDERS

APEX RIDERS is more than just a motorcycle racing group in Roblox's Drag Drive Simulator. It began as a group of friends who shared a passion for motorcycles, racing, and adventure. Over time, it grew into a community built on friendship, loyalty, respect, teamwork, and brotherhood.

Behind every race, tambike, event, and achievement were people who helped make APEX RIDERS what it became.

01 / SEPTEMBER 12, 2025

THE BEGINNING — WHERE IT ALL STARTED

APEX RIDERS was founded on September 12, 2025, by APEX_Yuji, also known as Kapitan Yuji. At first, the goal was simple: bring together people who enjoyed riding and racing in Drag Drive Simulator. As more members joined, the group slowly became something bigger. It was no longer just about winning races. It was about making friends, building connections, helping one another, and creating memories together.

02 / COMMUNITY GROWTH

BUILDING THE FAMILY

As APEX RIDERS grew, members began taking on responsibilities to help manage the community. The group developed a leadership structure that included an Owner and Co-Owners, Head Administrators and Administrators, Moderators, Racing Heads for Drag and Circuit, a Media Team, Recruiters, Uniform Designers, and the Apex Lady Riders team.

Each position had a purpose. Some members managed the community, while others organized races, welcomed new members, designed uniforms, and documented the group's activities. APEX RIDERS also established rules to encourage respect, discipline, loyalty, and clean racing.

03 / EVENTS & ACHIEVEMENTS

EVENTS, COLLABORATIONS, AND ACHIEVEMENTS

Throughout its journey, APEX RIDERS participated in activities, collaborations, and racing events. Memorable moments included collaborations with KSx, RBD, and AE; participation in The Isle of Bandung Race Event, where APEX members achieved first place and other placements; group tambikes and community rides; Christmas and Year-End celebrations; recruitment activities; and connections with other racing communities.

These experiences strengthened relationships among members and created memories worth keeping.

04 / LEADERSHIP TRANSITIONS

CHALLENGES AND CHANGES IN LEADERSHIP

Like every organization, APEX RIDERS experienced challenges. Some members became inactive because of school, personal responsibilities, or other priorities. Others left, while some accepted new responsibilities to keep the community running.

Leadership went through several transitions as different members stepped up to manage the clan and continue the responsibilities of previous leaders. Kapitan Yuji also considered stepping back and allowing other leaders to take responsibility for the community. These changes were not always easy, but they became part of APEX RIDERS' story.

05 / A QUIETER CHAPTER

THE INACTIVE PERIOD

Eventually, APEX RIDERS experienced a period of inactivity. Group rides became less frequent, conversations slowed down, and members focused more on their individual lives.

However, inactivity did not erase what members had experienced together. The friendships, achievements, laughter, and memories remained part of the community's history.

06 / SEPTEMBER 12, 2026

REUNITED — FIRST ANNIVERSARY

On September 12, 2026, APEX RIDERS celebrated its first anniversary with the theme REUNITED.

Despite the clan being inactive, former and current members came together for the anniversary tambike. It was a reminder that even when people go their separate ways, the memories they created remain.

The anniversary was not simply about celebrating one year of existence. It was about remembering where everything started, appreciating everyone who became part of the journey, and recognizing the people who helped build APEX RIDERS.

07 / THE LEGACY

THE LEGACY OF APEX RIDERS

From September 12, 2025, to its first anniversary on September 12, 2026, APEX RIDERS completed a journey filled with different people, experiences, and challenges.

Some members stayed until the end. Some moved on. Some became leaders, while others quietly supported the community behind the scenes. Every person who became part of APEX RIDERS contributed something to its story.

The real success of APEX RIDERS was never measured only by the number of members, trophies, or victories. It was measured by the friendships it created and the memories people would carry even after the community became inactive.

RULES & REGULATIONS

RULE 01

RESPECT EVERYONE

Be polite to members, admins, opponents, and other clans. No trash talk, bullying, or rude behavior.

RULE 02

BE RESPONSIBLE

Think before you act. Your behavior represents the whole team.

RULE 03

CONFLICT POLICY

  • DO NOT fight back if someone provokes you.
  • Report immediately to any Admin or Head Admin.
  • Must provide evidence: screenshots, clips, or proof of incident.
RULE 04

COMPLAINT CHANNEL

You may approach the Head Admin, any Admin, or Captain of Both Factions for concerns.

RULE 05

ACTIVITY RULE

  • Be active in chats, training, and scheduled events.
  • Notify your Captain if you will be inactive for days.
  • Improvement Focus: We encourage growth — skill-wise, attitude-wise, and teamwork-wise.
